# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming, be respectful, assume good intent, give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the people who use JourneyGuard. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language and publishing others' private information are not tolerated, in the repository or in any community space connected to it.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that do not follow this code, and may ban contributors for behaviour they consider inappropriate.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through the contact form at [nxdpro.com](https://www.nxdpro.com). Reports are handled confidentially.
